# Complaint Handling Policy

Version: 0.1.0
Status: Demonstration policy—not legal guidance

## Triage

- Route credit reporting complaints to the Credit Reporting team.
- Route debt collection complaints to the Collections Resolution team.
- Route money transfer and payment complaints to the Payments team.
- Route all other complaints to General Resolution.

## Escalation

- Flag a complaint for priority review when the narrative describes imminent foreclosure, active identity theft, inability to access essential funds, or threats of legal action.
- A low-confidence classification must be reviewed before assignment.
- Recommendations must cite both the complaint evidence and the policy passage that supports the proposed action.

## Human approval

The system may recommend a route, priority, and response checklist. A human operator must approve or edit the recommendation. The demonstration must not characterize its output as legal advice.
